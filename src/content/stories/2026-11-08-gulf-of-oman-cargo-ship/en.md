US Central Command, known as CENTCOM, says a US military aircraft fired on and disabled a cargo ship in the Gulf of Oman on Saturday, November 7. The command says the ship, the St. Kitts-flagged M/V Huda, had tried to leave the region after visiting an Iranian port.

## What Central Command says

According to CENTCOM, the civilian crew ignored repeated warnings and tried several times to violate the US naval blockade of Iran. The command says its forces directed the crew to abandon the targeted spaces, and the aircraft then fired precision munitions at the ship's stern. A video posted by CENTCOM shows an explosion at the stern, according to DW.

CENTCOM's statement does not say whether anyone was hurt. No source in this report gives Iran's position on the M/V Huda.

The command also says that since July US forces have redirected 147 commercial vessels that complied with the blockade and disabled five merchant ships that refused. It says it has previously destroyed 10 Iranian oil tankers that it links to a shadow network of the Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps. CENTCOM estimates the blockade has kept more than $25 billion in Iranian crude oil from being exported. It also announced a task force against Iran's movement of advanced weapons and other sanctioned material over land borders and through the Caspian Sea.

## Other reports from the area

The UAE National Guard said early on Sunday that 22 people had been rescued from a cargo ship in the gulf. None of the sources says these people came from the M/V Huda.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said in a statement that oil tankers had hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire. It said the ships were using an unauthorised route. The sources disagree on the number, with Euronews and AP reporting two tankers and DW reporting three. Iranian state media also reported explosions on Qeshm Island and in the port of Jask, and the cause was not immediately clear, according to AP.

The incident comes as President Donald Trump says he is considering a new wave of strikes on Iran after the US midterm elections held on November 3. He said on Friday that US forces had carried out limited strikes on an Iranian air defense system and described them as minor. The war is more than eight months old, and AP says diplomacy to end it has stalled.
