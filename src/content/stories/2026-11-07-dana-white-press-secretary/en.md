President Donald Trump has offered Dana White, the president and CEO of the Ultimate Fighting Championship, the job of White House press secretary. He made the offer in a post on Truth Social on Saturday.

"I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States," Trump wrote. "Dana, please take the job!" He added that White should bring some UFC fighters along as assistants, "even though some are not from our country." In his words, "We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left."

## What is not clear

It is not known whether the offer is genuine. The BBC says it is unclear, and it has asked the White House and TKO Holdings, the parent company of the UFC, for comment. CNBC reports that neither the White House nor the UFC responded right away.

After the post, White shared an Instagram story showing himself behind the lectern in the White House briefing room, with the caption "On to the next chapter." CNBC and the BBC both say it is unclear whether White has accepted or is joking. The Jerusalem Post reports that White has said before that he has no political ambitions and no interest in a government job.

## Why the job is open

The press secretary post has been vacant since October 16, when Katie Zacharia left after less than a week. Trump told reporters that he had suggested she quit after her first briefing, in which she spent six minutes in front of reporters and took questions from three of them.

The offer follows a week of changes in Washington. Democrats won control of the House and Senate in the midterm elections, and on Friday Trump announced that deputy chief of staff Stephen Miller would replace Susie Wiles as chief of staff.

## A long friendship

White has run the UFC since 2001 as president and since 2023 as CEO. He and Trump have been friends for many years. Trump hosted UFC events at his Atlantic City casinos in 2001, when the sport struggled to find venues, and White spoke at the Republican National Conventions in 2016, 2020 and 2024. In June, around Trump's 80th birthday, the UFC held an event called UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn.
