Lionel Messi has become the first player in MLS history to win the Golden Boot in two seasons in a row. The Inter Miami forward scored 26 goals in 31 appearances this season, four clear of FC Dallas striker Musa, according to BBC Sport. Al Jazeera puts Musa second with 22.

Messi is only the sixth player to win the award twice. He also set up 17 goals, and his 43 goal contributions were again the best in the league, BBC Sport reports. Al Jazeera adds that he scored 29 goals the season before, which made him the second player in league history to reach at least 25 goals in back-to-back years.

## A quiet final day

Messi did not score on the last day of the regular season, but he set up the first two goals from corners in a 3-3 draw with Charlotte, according to Al Jazeera. He also hit the post in stoppage time. Miami led 3-0 in the first half before Charlotte came back to level in the 90th minute.

## What comes next

Miami, the defending MLS Cup champions, finished second in the Eastern Conference on 60 points. Nashville won the East with 77 points, which Al Jazeera describes as an all-time MLS record for a season. The old mark of 74 was set by Miami in 2024. The draw still earned Miami home advantage in the first two playoff rounds.

The playoffs begin on November 19, and Miami open against Columbus. BBC Sport says the first round is played over three games. Messi may add another honour soon, as BBC Sport notes he could become the first three-time winner of the Landon Donovan MLS MVP award after winning it in 2024 and 2025.

Al Jazeera also reports that Messi recently retired from international football and signed a contract extension with Miami through 2028.
