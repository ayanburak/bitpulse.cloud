## The story

The English body has about 620 words and the Turkish body about 550. The image is a toy-shop display case with boxed Switch consoles and games, by Ominae, from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0. The main sources are Nintendo's results release and its supplementary slides of 5 November 2026.

- The writer found that Nintendo's documents do not mention cartridges or game-key cards, and the story says so. It computed yen amounts from Nintendo's shares and took the Switch 2 prior-year figure from games.gg.
- The fact checker confirmed the Switch 2 figures in Nintendo's slides and dropped the games.gg attribution for them. It also quoted Nintendo's own reasons for the rise, including the weak yen, and fixed the wording on the tariff refund. It checked the computed amounts and confirmed that the documents never mention cards or cartridges.
- The editor cut the computed yen amounts and several totals that a general reader cannot feel, rewrote the summary so it no longer repeats the headline, and brought the Macronix recap closer to that story's wording.
- The final checker changed the English headline from "Nintendo Now Sells 60.7% of Its Game Software Digitally". That version read like a share of units and said "now", although the latest quarter was 59.8%. It added Nintendo's support page as the source for the game-key card definition, and changed "quoted" to "said", because the Macronix story does not quote the analyst. It also changed a closing line that contradicted an earlier sentence. Its search for newer reports found no Nintendo comment on cartridges or cards. It found a US Circana figure on game-key card spending and a report that Nintendo counts game-key cards as physical sales, both possible material for a later story.
- The main agent removed "about" before the exact 54.5% figure in both languages.
- The social writer's first commit had an X post of 290 characters. CI failed on the draft branch, and the next commit shortened the post. The main agent compared every sentence of social.yaml with the story and found no new claim. One page uses Nintendo's logo, because no image of the results exists.

No return to the writer was needed. Each role was told that a sentence summarising an earlier Bitpulse story is checked against that story, and no such error reached the main agent this time.
