## The story

The English version has about 1,980 words and the Turkish version about 1,540, counted on the source files. The Turkish hook is the guide on AI use for lawyers from the Union of Turkish Bar Associations, read in its primary PDF. No Turkish court case was found, so none is claimed.

The first fact check returned the story. The opening stated the New York lawyer's account as fact, although the court called his explanations "shifting and contradictory" and found bad faith. The guide was cited only through a news page, and one line presented an inference as the finding of a paper. A new writer rebuilt the opening on the screenshots in the court record, attributed the rest to the lawyer and added the guide. The second fact check passed the changes with one correction. One of the two allowed returns was used.

The editor moved the court chronology out of the opening. The final checker approved the story after correcting both languages. It restored the court filing and the colleague to the opening, made the moved paragraph clear where it now sits, narrowed one overbroad statement, added the court's rejection of the lawyer's account, changed "fictional" to "unknown" to match Anthropic's wording, and added a Hukuki Haber source for the August date. In Turkish it also restored a "research suggests" hedge and changed the title to "Yapay Zekâ". That spelling was kept because earlier Turkish stories use it, while the slug stays yapay-zeka-neden-uydurur, the plain form most readers type.

## What went wrong

- The writer took the opening from the lawyer's declaration and did not compare it with the court's findings, which cost one return and a second fact check, about 77,000 tokens. A court record has to be read for what the court found, and not only for what a party said.
- The editor moved fact-checked text, which added work to the final check, and the final checker had to restore part of it. The story was approved, but the most exposed claims changed at the last step.
- The first writer reported the word counts without the tests, because its worktree had no node_modules. The main agent linked them in and ran the tests after each role. The link was removed with the worktree and never committed.
- The final check changed the Turkish title from the brief's "Yapay Zeka" form without flagging it as a search question. The main agent reviewed it after the fact.
- The corrections page on the proposal branch can list only a title and dates, because stories have no field for a correction note. The proposal says so.
- The leftover worktree at /tmp/bitpulse-run on proposal/search-and-sharing-metadata is still there. It is outside the repository and affects nothing.
