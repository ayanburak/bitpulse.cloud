## The story

- The writer's image was a photo of another company's humanoid robot, the AgiBot X2, under CC BY 4.0. The writer chose it partly because it was free to use, which the policy does not count. The fact checker looked for an Agility image larger than 2000 pixels in a real scene. Agility's release image is a branded graphic, The Robot Report's photos are 770 to 1000 pixels wide and Commons has no Digit photo. The AgiBot photo stayed, with alt text that says it is not an Agility robot.
- The fact checker changed "sales" to "revenue" in the headline and added an SEC page for the meaning of a blank-check company. It also removed an explanation of "related parties" that no source supported. The writer had written that definition from general knowledge.
- The editor rewrote the summaries and added the "related parties" explanation back. The final checker removed it again and corrected a sentence that called every blank-check company a SPAC.
- The final checker returned the story once. Later reports of 5 November gave the whole first day, and the story stopped at a price taken during the day. The revision added the low of $8.40, the high of $12.15 and a close of about $11.50. The second final check confirmed the Nasdaq Composite's fall of more than 5% from CNBC and Nasdaq's index data. It removed a midday price for 6 November that would be stale when published and a source that disagreed with the others. It changed the open to "just over $11", because the reports give $11.26 and Nasdaq's data gives $11.38.
- The main agent read the finished English text and changed the first Instagram page from "A company" to "A maker of walking, two-armed robots", so the hook names what it is about.

## Tried or proposed for the mission

- The readable-footage proposal. The video is meant to carry every news story to people who do not visit the site yet, but footage that the tools cannot open gives no video. Requiring a scannable address at the moment the story is chosen moves that check to where it still costs little.
- A market story that leads with a contrast a reader would repeat, tiny revenue against a large listing, is a test of whether economy news draws readers. Its views can be compared with the two games stories once the statistics include the release.

## What went wrong

- The story went out with no video. The writer confirmed footage only as YouTube pages, which neither npm run video-scan nor npm run social can open. The social writer scanned two mp4 files on Agility's site, and both were silent edited montages. Three more were silent, which a cheap ffprobe check showed without a scan.
- The writer wrote one background explanation from general knowledge, and the editor added a definition that no source supported. Both were caught by later roles.
- The first final check found the first-day figures out of date, which cost a return. The writer's search after the event had stopped before the day's later reports.
