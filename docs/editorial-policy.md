# Editorial policy

These rules apply to everything published on Bitpulse.

## Scope

Bitpulse is a news site that explains technology, science, games, culture and the economy to curious general readers. A story is welcome whenever it happened, including today, as long as its subject is one of these. Explainers answer lasting questions under the same rules. How stories sound is defined in docs/style-guide.md.

There are five categories: Economy, Technology, Science, Games and Culture. Their Turkish names are Ekonomi, Teknoloji, Bilim, Oyun and Kültür, and their shared keys are economy, technology, science, games and culture.

Each category has a focus. Technology covers artificial intelligence, robots, devices, chips, vehicles, aviation and the internet. Science covers space, nature and wildlife, health and medical research, physics and the Earth, and a natural event can be a science story when the story is about the event itself and not the harm it caused. Games covers new games, trailers, consoles and hardware, studios and players. Culture covers film, television, music, books, internet culture and the arts. Economy covers companies, markets, prices, trade and the wider economy.

Party politics, elections, government disputes, foreign policy, war and sports results are not covered. Stories whose subject is harm to people, such as crime, violence, accidents and disasters, are not covered either. A story is not excluded only because a government, a law or a controversy plays a part in it.

## Publishing rhythm

The target is five stories per day. There are no issues, numbered editions or fixed slots. Quality takes priority over the target, so a weak story is not published to fill a gap.

## Sources

Every factual claim is traceable to a named source, including background facts, and the sources are shown with the article. Primary sources are preferred, such as official announcements, company filings and research papers. Claims from a single weak source are not reported as fact. Text is written from the facts and is not copied from the sources.

A source counts only if its page was opened and read. Anything attributed to a person or a publication must appear on the cited page. Every story is checked against its sources by someone other than its writer before it is published.

## Economy stories

Economy stories report what happened. They never tell readers to buy, sell or hold anything, and they do not predict prices as if the outcome were known. Bitpulse does not give investment advice.

## Corrections

Mistakes are fixed openly. A corrected article shows a dated correction note that says what was wrong. Silent changes to facts are not allowed.

## Images

Every story has an image, and the first choice is an image directly tied to the story. Any image may be used, including press images, film stills, game screenshots, trailer frames and news agency photos, as long as its owner and its source are shown next to it. When an image has no open license, the license field gives its terms as the source states them and links to the source page. Whether an image is free to use is never a reason to choose or drop a story.

A general image about the topic must be honest about what it shows, so its description names what is really in the frame and it never suggests a person or a thing was part of the event when it was not.

An image fills its whole frame with a real scene. A cut out object on a white, plain or transparent background is not used, because it breaks the look of the pages. Product photos work only when the product sits in a real setting.

Images are JPEG or PNG in landscape format. The original must be sharp and at least 1200 pixels wide. The stored copy keeps the original width up to 2000 pixels and is never enlarged, so it stays clear without slowing the page. An image of the story's own subject at 1200 pixels is better than a larger image of something else.

Social posts use images the same way. An image on a social post shows the event, the people, the place or the product of the story itself, and a general or stock image is not used there. Each image is credited to its owner in the post, and when it was taken from a news report, the outlet is named as well. Social images are linked by their address and never stored in this repository or shown on the site. A social video is footage of the event of the story, or for an economy story, footage of what the story is about. A slow event may be sped up, and the caption says so. It is credited to its owner in the caption and linked by its address.

## Disclosure and bylines

This repository states openly that the stories are chosen by the project owner and researched with the help of AI. The live website and the social posts do not state this. The footer links to this repository. Articles have no byline, and the site has no author pages, author boxes, team sections or personal names.

## Languages

Each story is published in English and Turkish with the same facts and the same sources. Each language version is written to read naturally and is not a word for word translation.
