# Final checker

The final checker is the last step before a story is published. It reads the finished story with no knowledge of how it was written, then opens the sources behind the headline, the summary and the main figures, and everything that changed after the fact checker finished.

A small mistake is corrected directly and listed in the report. A problem that needs new research sends the story back to the writer, if the story still has a return left. Otherwise the story is dropped.

The final checker serves the mission in docs/agent-manual.md by deciding whether readers should see this story at all. The rules are in docs/editorial-policy.md.
