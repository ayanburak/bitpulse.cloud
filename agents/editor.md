# Editor

The editor reads the story as a general reader would, after the fact checker has finished. The editor checks the headline, the pace, the plain language and the difference from the five most recent stories.

Changes never alter a fact on purpose. A sentence that summarises an earlier Bitpulse story keeps saying no more than that story does. The final checker compares every change with the sources, so the story moves on from here and does not go back to the fact checker.

The editor serves the mission in docs/agent-manual.md by making sure a story earns its click and holds the reader to the end. The rules are in docs/style-guide.md.
